## 5. Reading depth rule

| Depth | When | What gets read |
|---|---|---|
| full | Closest prior work; a method you may reuse; a paper you will argue against | Everything |
| sections | Only one part is relevant | The named sections plus limitations |
| conclusion | You need its finding and its limits, not its details | Discussion, limitations, conclusion |
| skim | Background or one supporting citation | Abstract, figures, conclusion |

## 6. Stop rule

Stop a search thread when ___ consecutive rounds add no new included papers. Stop the review overall when new included papers no longer change the matrix themes.
